How it works
  1. Add your song (MP3, WAV or M4A) and choose the part you want. Clips of 10 to 30 seconds work best.
  2. Add the lyrics. Type the song title and artist and press Find lyrics and sync, or type or paste the lyrics yourself.
  3. Check the sync. Press Play. If words are off, use Sync with AI or Sync by tapping, then fix single words on the word timeline.
  4. Pick a look and press Save video.

Tip: press the space bar to play or pause. While you are syncing by tapping, the space bar taps instead. Press ? for all keyboard shortcuts. Your work is autosaved in this browser, so an accidental refresh can be recovered (add the song again).

Questions and answers

Are my song and pictures uploaded anywhere?

No. Your song, pictures and finished videos are processed in your browser and stay on your device. The few things that do go online are listed under Privacy.

Does it work offline?

Not fully. Fonts, the lyrics lookup, AI sync and the library that saves MP4 or WebM files all load from the internet.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge on a computer works best. Other browsers may save videos with a simpler method that can be slower or lower quality. The 3D camera style needs WebGL.

Why is saving a video slow?

The page draws every frame one by one so the result is smooth. Longer clips and phones take longer. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

The words are early or late.

If every word is off by the same amount, change Lyrics timing offset in step 2, or use Shift all words in Fine-tune timing. For single words, drag them on the word timeline.

No lyrics were found.

Check the spelling and add the artist. Very new or rare songs may not be listed. You can paste the lyrics and use Sync with AI or Sync by tapping instead.

Can I use any song?

Use only music and lyrics you have the right to use. Sharing a video that contains copyrighted music or lyrics can be blocked or removed by the platform you post it on. This is general information, not legal advice.

Privacy, credits and rights

There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics, and nothing you make is stored on a server.

What goes online:

  • The song title and artist you type are sent to the lyrics services (LRCLIB, and Lyrica or your own server) when you press Find lyrics and sync.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address as it does for any web font.
  • AI sync downloads a speech model once (about 75 MB or 250 MB, your choice) and its code libraries. Your audio is analysed on your device.
  • Saving MP4 or WebM loads a small video library from jsDelivr.
  • Beat Type asks before it uses each of these services for the first time. If you say no, it uses on-device alternatives where they exist. Autosave keeps lyrics, timings and settings in this browser’s local storage only (never the song or pictures).

Credits: lyric timing data from LRCLIB and Lyrica. Speech recognition uses OpenAI Whisper models through Transformers.js. Fonts: Fredoka, Fraunces, Anton, Allura and Playfair Display from Google Fonts.

Rights: songs and lyrics belong to their owners. Beat Type does not store, provide or own any lyrics or music.
